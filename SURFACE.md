# Studio and player

Modes: Operate for the editor; Experience for playback. This extends the pinned existing artifact. The user confirmed the side-panel/live-preview concept and delegated implementation tools before authorizing work.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A Persian scene-editing workspace surrounding the preserved presentation. Editing is direct and page-specific; no new marketing wrapper.

OWN-WORLD: Extracted YekanX, charcoal text, white workspace and quiet gray rails, one gold selection/action accent. Presentation imagery and original animation own the visual richness.

STORY: Choose one of two decks, select a page, edit text or replace its media, preview immediately, save locally and present. The original artifact remains available independently.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A thin command bar above a right-hand chapter menu with current-category pages across the top, central live stage, and left-hand inspector. Save state stays visible. The player keeps the original caption/device composition, manual previous/play/next controls and a per-video loop toggle, without a timeline or bottom flow map.

FORM: User-pinned side-panel editor with live preview; no direction seed is needed for this specific extension of an established surface. Signature interaction: selecting a scene updates both inspector and the deterministic original animation; a paused scene freezes timers and visual animation together. Videos autoplay muted and stay on their page until a navigation action. Text and media have move and resize handles in editing mode, saved separately for desktop/mobile.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
